# 100-car catalog expansion

Status: owner approved 8 Secrets, 2026-10-20. The expansion adds 100 original, reference-inspired
rollable identities to the existing shared-physics skin pipeline. It does not copy meshes,
textures, names, logos, or extracted assets from another game.

The permanent source of truth is `src/shared/CarExpansionManifest.luau`. Each entry also has
its own snake-case module in `src/shared/Cars/`; the module's `Id`, `Chance`, `Rarity`,
`SpeedMulti`, `Order`, and `Model` are save/catalog contracts and must not be renamed or reused.

## Skin pipeline

Expansion skins are procedural, Roblox-ready authored placeholders generated by
`Shared/DraftCarModels` until Blender-authored exports are supplied. They are cosmetic only:
the invisible shared chassis, seat, wheels, collision group, and physics remain authoritative.
Generated parts are anchored in the template, then cloned, welded, non-colliding, massless,
and attached by `CarAppearance` in the same path as imported skins. Family palettes and
silhouette recipes are recorded in the manifest so each identity is deterministic.

The final art pass must replace each generated template with its Blender source, preview,
export, and license note under `drafts/cars/<id>/`, following the root `CAR_MODELING_WORKFLOW.md`.
The similarly named `docs/CAR_MODELING_WORKFLOW.md` is an expansion checklist, not a replacement
for the established root workflow.

## Car icons and model detail (owner, 2026-10-20)

- Every Index tile and every Backpack Cars tile shows the car's own model (a ViewportFrame of its
  `ReplicatedStorage.CarModels` template, three-quarter front view), never a shared fallback icon. An
  undiscovered Index tile shows the same model as a black silhouette with "?". The old 11-cell icon atlas
  stays only where no model exists.
- Expansion models get a detail pass in Blender (`tools/build_expansion_cars.py`): family-specific trim
  (lights, grilles, spoilers, roof gear, fins, wings, ornaments) so no two cars in a family share a silhouette.
  Size limits, the shared chassis, collision and physics are unchanged.
- Owner 2026-10-20: sharper edges and more unique shapes. Every expansion body is a hard-edged shell: low block,
  narrower upper deck with chamfered shoulder wedges, wedge nose, an id-picked tail (fastback, Kamm or ducktail),
  tapered glasshouse, slit lamps, and id-seeded proportions, cabin placement and paint pattern. Small round trim
  became blocks or wedges. The 7 Draft05 MeshPart cars (`assets/studio/CarModels.rbxm`) are unchanged until their
  meshes are re-made and uploaded by hand.
