# 100-car expansion source note

This folder tracks the 100-entry expansion roster while the final Blender art is authored.
The current runtime skin source is the deterministic procedural builder in
`src/shared/DraftCarModels.luau`; it is original Roblox geometry and is not extracted from
another game. Each permanent catalog module and the full family/chance/speed manifest live
under `src/shared/`.

Finalization still requires one Blender source, preview, Roblox export, and originality/license
note per car, imported and validated in five waves of 20 under `docs/CAR_MODELING_WORKFLOW.md`.
This draft is therefore not an assertion that the final art deliverables are complete.
