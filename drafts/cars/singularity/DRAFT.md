## Look
Original wedge hypercar (no brand, logo or third-party asset): knife-edge nose and splitter with an ember lip, chamfered
shoulders, raked low canopy, angled slit headlights, rear haunches with side intakes, swan-neck wing with sharp
endplates, diffuser fins and a full-width light blade. The black hole sits in the engine bay: a black core inside an
ember accretion disc and a pale halo. Wheels are black with glowing ember rims.
